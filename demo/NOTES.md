# Keep demo notes (native-app path)

Keep is Swift. Surfaces in the composition are rebuilt in the browser from
the app's own materials, Colosseum style:

- Sky engine: `composition/src/sky.js` + `solar.js` port `DaySceneView`,
  `SkyAppearance`, `WeatherParticles`, and Delhi GPS solar math (28.61, 77.21,
  11 Sep 2026). Canvas 2D, seeded SplitMix64, no Swift PNG plates.
- Extra chrome from `MenuBarView.swift`. Fraunces stands in for New York,
  Inter for SF.
- Fixtures: Ship the demo, Intro Call in 2h.

- Extra in the film is Apple liquid glass (SDF bevel displacement + saturate
  blur), not the opaque Swift extra. Search pill is a second glass lens.
  Cut is 18s: frozen still, type, overlay on a turning day.

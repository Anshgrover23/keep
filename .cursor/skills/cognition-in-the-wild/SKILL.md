# Cognition in the Wild

Source: Edwin Hutchins, *Cognition in the Wild* (1995). Navigation on a Navy ship. Computation is in people, tools, paper, talk, and the space. Not only inside a skull.

## Steal

Do not ask whether Keep is smart. Ask where the work of “what is today” lives. Calendar in EventKit, sky in SolarEngine, line in the extra, glance on the glass. The unit of analysis is the desk.

If you centralize everything into a chat agent, you hide the tools that already hold the answer.

## Keep checks

* Overlay is a public representation on the desk, like a chart on the bridge.
* Do not replace the sky with a paragraph from a model.
* Lab drives the same production path so the “ship” is not a second vessel.

## Post shape

Hutchins watched a ship think with maps and people, not a single genius.

Keep is that for a Mac. The day is computed across wallpaper, extra, and Calendar. Not inside a mascot.

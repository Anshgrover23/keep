# Original content

Load this for Keep posts. Load one source skill after you name the idea. Do not load every source skill at once.

## Refuse

* "Liquid Glass is beautiful."
* Summarizing a book you did not use while shipping.
* Hunting the least read book. Obscurity is not interest.
* Atomic Habits, Zero to One, Clean Code as the hook.

## Formula (verbatim)

**Obscure idea + something you're building + your observation = original content**

## Strategy (verbatim)

**Read things that are old, obscure, technical, or outside your field, then connect them to things you actually care about.**

The interesting content isn't:

> "Liquid Glass is beautiful."

It's more like:

> "I was reading about an interface principle from decades ago and suddenly understood why Liquid Glass feels different."

That's much more personal because **the observation came from your own reading and experience**.

## Keep example (verbatim shape)

> I spent years thinking good UI meant making things look clean.
>
> Then I started building native apps.
>
> I realized good UI is less about decoration and more about teaching the user what they can do without explaining it.
>
> That changed how I think about SwiftUI, web interfaces, and even Apple's approach to Liquid Glass.

## Workflow

1. Name the live artifact (glass cycle mp4, extra, wallpaper, smash K).
2. Name one observation from that artifact. If you cannot point at a frame, stop.
3. Pick one source skill. Steal one principle, not a bibliography.
4. Write: what you used to think. What you built. What broke that thought.
5. Attach the video or still. No hashtag dump. Prose copy still applies (no hyphen or dash in running copy).

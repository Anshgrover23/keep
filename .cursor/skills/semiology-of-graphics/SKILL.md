# Semiology of graphics

Source: Jacques Bertin, *Semiology of Graphics* (1967). A graphic is a system of marks. Position, size, value, texture, color, orientation, shape. Efficiency is how fast the eye reads the intended question.

## Steal

One question, one mark family. Two suns ask two questions. Rainbow dispersion on glass asks a third.

Keep overlay: at most two lines. That is two marks on the sky, not a dashboard.

## Keep checks

* Sun disc is the mark for time of day. Do not add a white core inside the word Keep.
* Weather is the sky field, not extra icons unless asked.
* Film captions compete with the serif Keep. Mute first.

## Post shape

Bertin treated graphics as a language of marks.

I had two suns on one pill. That is two sentences. The sky only needed one.

# The Illusion of Life

Source: Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston, *The Illusion of Life: Disney Animation* (1981). Twelve principles from Disney’s 1930s craft. Physics plus emotional timing.

## Twelve (use the ones that bite)

1. Squash and stretch
2. Anticipation
3. Staging
4. Straight ahead and pose to pose
5. Follow through and overlapping action
6. Slow in and slow out
7. Arc
8. Secondary action
9. Timing
10. Exaggeration
11. Solid drawing
12. Appeal

## Keep checks

* Sun path is an arc. Linear civil time so noon is a pose, not a skipped inbetween.
* Smash K: anticipation, then follow through of pieces, then the extra as overlapping action.
* Zoom on glass cycle is slow in slow out. Do not punch so hard Keep becomes unreadable.
* Staging: one idea per frame. Fake interior sun failed staging.
* Exaggeration: glassCycle bodyScale 1.55 is for a small X box. Wallpaper stays 1.

## Post shape

Disney’s book is about making drawings obey weight.

The sun has to travel an arc through the glass.

When I eased the clock, noon vanished. That was bad timing, not bad taste.

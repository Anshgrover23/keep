# Snow White language

Source: Hartmut Esslinger and Frog for Apple (contest then IIc). In house later as Apple Industrial Design Group. Snow White: deep grooves in plastic, unified “Fog” then “Platinum”. Copied by PC clones, then abandoned for smaller devices.

## Steal

A design language is a family of physical moves, not a gradient. Liquid Glass is a family of refraction, clear sampling, tint as a last resort. If Keep paints its own language on top (RGB split, full pill tint), it is a clone groove, not Apple’s.

Unify the promo and the product sky. Two palettes is two languages.

## Keep checks

* `.clear` glass. No even yellow wash.
* Cream Analog K is Keep’s object language, like Fog was Apple’s.

## Post shape

Snow White was grooves you could feel on the IIc.

Liquid Glass is grooves in light.

I stopped etching a fake highlight and let the real sun cut the edge.

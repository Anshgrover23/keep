# Art and visual perception

Source: Rudolf Arnheim, *Art and Visual Perception* (1954). Gestalt for makers. Figure and ground, balance, simplicity, dynamics. The eye organizes, it does not inventory pixels.

## Steal

If Keep (the word) and the sun are both figure, the glass fails as ground. Noon pass works when the sun is figure, the pill is a lens in ground, and Keep stays a quiet third.

Balance is not centering everything. A low left dawn sun is a directed force.

## Keep checks

* Overlay titles: regular serif, readable on weather. Not ultralight lost in ground.
* Glass cycle pill offset: sun may sit above, then pass. Empty side crop is balance, not more UI.

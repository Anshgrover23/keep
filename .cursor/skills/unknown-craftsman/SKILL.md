# The Unknown Craftsman

Source: Soetsu Yanagi, *The Unknown Craftsman*. Mingei. Beauty in unsigned useful things. Pattern from making, not from a signature.

## Steal

Keep should feel thrown, like a bowl used every day. The Analog cream K is enough. Do not put Introducing Keep on the wallpaper. Do not caption the smash.

A craftsman repeats a form until it is quiet. The sky loop is that repetition.

## Keep checks

* No Hallmark. No mascot explaining the day.
* Demo film: the cursor and the K are craft. Voiceover is optional.

## Post shape

Yanagi cared about pots nobody signed.

I am not posting a logo reveal.

I am posting a day that will run unsigned on a desk.

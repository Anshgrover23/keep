# The Eyes of the Skin

Source: Juhani Pallasmaa, *The Eyes of the Skin* (1996). Architecture lost touch when it became retinal. Hands, temperature, peripheral vision. The body knows the room before the diagram does.

## Steal

CSS glass is retinal. `NSGlassEffectView` and SwiftUI `.glassEffect(.clear)` are closer to a material you could almost tap. Record the native pass. Do not substitute a browser fake for the post.

Peripheral sky matters more than a hero icon in the extra.

## Keep checks

* Wallpaper under icons is bodily: you live in it.
* Reduce Motion is a body setting. Honor it.
* Interactive glass in the promo: cursor as a finger on the lens. Product: do not require a finger on the wallpaper.

## Post shape

Pallasmaa said we have been designing for the retina.

I stopped faking glass in CSS.

I recorded Apple’s glass with Keep’s sky behind it. That is closer to skin than to a mock.

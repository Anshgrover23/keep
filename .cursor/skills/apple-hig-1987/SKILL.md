# Apple HIG 1987

Source: *Human Interface Guidelines: The Apple Desktop Interface* (Addison Wesley, 1987). Desktop is the primary metaphor. Interface is all communication, not only pixels.

Scan: https://media.512pixels.net/downloads/files/1987-HIG.pdf

1992 Macintosh HIG is the next edition. Use 1987 for Lisa and Macintosh roots. Do not treat WWDC slides as this book.

## Ten principles (apply, do not recite)

1. **Metaphors from the real world.** Concrete, then loose. Desktop is a surface. Do not force every Keep control to be a physical object.
2. **Direct manipulation.** Physical action, physical result. Sky moves because the day moves. Fake interior sun is not a result.
3. **See and point, not remember and type.** Noun then verb. Recognition over recall. Wallpaper shows today. Do not make them remember five catalogues.
4. **Consistency.** One coherent way. Prefer the Mac pattern over a clever Keep only gadget.
5. **WYSIWYG.** No secrets, no abstract command that only promises a later picture. The desktop they see is the product.
6. **User control.** User initiates. Warn on risky, then allow. Invitational permissions. Computer is not protector.
7. **Feedback and dialog.** Immediate, brief, user vocabulary. No HTTP codes. No type names.
8. **Forgiveness.** Exploration. Reversible. Alerts rare. A barrage of alerts means the design is wrong.
9. **Perceived stability.** Menu bar and wallpaper stay as landmarks while content changes. Unavailable actions dim, they do not vanish.
10. **Aesthetic integrity.** Different things look different. User may make a mess. The app may not. Graphics communicate, they do not dazzle.

## Graphic rule from the book

Good design must communicate, not just dazzle. It must inform, not just impress.

Begin color work in black and white. Limit color on standard chrome.

## Keep checks

* Extra is see and point. Field is the noun. Sending is the verb.
* Wallpaper is the stable desktop surface. Overlay is content, not a new metaphor.
* Liquid Glass samples the sky. That is WYSIWYG for light. A painted caustic is a secret.

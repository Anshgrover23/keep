# Ecological perception

Source: James J. Gibson, *The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception* (1979). Perception is for action. The unit is organism plus environment. Affordances are what the environment offers that animal.

You do not first see centimeters then infer a step. You see climbable or not.

## Steal

Information is in the optic array, not a poor inner picture that needs decoration. Keep’s sky is an optic array that already changes with solar time. Glass should let that array through.

A second painted sun is not an affordance. It is a cartoon of light.

## Keep checks

* Clear glass, no tint wash that recolors the whole day.
* Caustic only where the real disc overlaps the pill.
* Interactive glass: light that tracks is an affordance of a lens. A blob at stadium inset is not.

## Post shape

Gibson said we see what we can do, not a snapshot that we then interpret.

The pill affords looking through. When I drew a highlight inside Keep, I broke that. The real sun on the rim restored it.

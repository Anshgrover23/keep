# Designing for people

Sources: Henry Dreyfuss, *Designing for People* (1955). *The Measure of Man* (body data for reach and comfort).

## Joe and Josephine

Design for the humans who actually use the object, not the purchaser who never sits in the chair. Percentiles of reach, sight line, fatigue. If it fails at the body, styling will not save it.

## Keep checks

* Operator is the person at the notebook all day. Extra in the menu bar is reach. Wallpaper is the wall they already face.
* Do not sell Keep to an exec who never opens the extra. Sell a desk that already knows today.
* Glass cycle is a promo at sitting distance, not a keynote IMAX.

## Post shape

Dreyfuss drew Joe and Josephine before he drew the product.

I am building for the person who has to defend the Mac in the next budget cycle.

They already live in the menu bar. That is the reach.

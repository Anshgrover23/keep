# Design in architecture

Source: Geoffrey Broadbent, *Design in Architecture: Architecture and the Human Sciences* (1973). Methods, models, Gestalt, cybernetics, CAD as a then new tool. Architecture as environmental process, not a facade.

## Steal

App navigation is spatial. A settings sheet is a room. The extra is a small room you can enter without leaving the hall. The wallpaper is the hall you always occupy.

Do not add a second hall (another dashboard window) unless they ask.

## Keep checks

* Lab is a workshop off the hall, same weather pipe.
* Onboarding is a doorway, not a building tour.
* Glass cycle window is a gallery. Product is still the hall.

## Post shape

Broadbent treated buildings as environments you move through.

Keep has a hall (the day) and a small room (the extra).

I refuse a lobby full of feature doors.
